# Graviton - Black Hole Esper Addon for Re:AcademyCraft 1.5.0

Adds the **Graviton (Black Hole)** Esper Category to Re:AcademyCraft on Minecraft 1.20.1 Forge.

## Skills Included:
1. **Level 1: Gravitational Pull (Instant)** - Yanks target enemies violently toward your focal point.
2. **Level 2: Event Horizon Field (Toggle)** - Generates an oppressive personal gravity field. Incoming projectiles are smashed downward into the ground; enemies are slowed and continuously crushed.
3. **Level 3: Gravitational Repulsion (Instant)** - Inverts gravity outward in all directions, blasting away surrounding attackers with a supersonic shockwave.
4. **Level 4: Micro Singularity (Toggle/Channel)** - Fires a traveling miniature black hole forward that vacuums up nearby mobs and projectiles along its flight path before imploding.
5. **Level 5: Supermassive Black Hole (Ultimate)** - Summons a cosmic Black Hole complete with an Event Horizon, a swirling Accretion Disk, extreme gravitational suction within 24 blocks, and a cataclysmic singularity collapse.

## How to Get the Ability in Game:
1. Put the compiled `.jar` file into your Minecraft `mods` folder along with `Re:AcademyCraft 1.5.0` and `Forge 47.4.10+`.
2. Craft or spawn the **Singularity Factor** (`blackhole:blackhole_factor`).
3. Place it into the **Ability Developer** to unlock the Graviton category!
